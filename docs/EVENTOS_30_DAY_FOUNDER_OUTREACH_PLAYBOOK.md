## 0. The Core Strategy in One Line

You are not "selling software." You are personally rescuing 25 boutique agency owners from WhatsApp group chaos, Excel budget errors, and awkward vendor-advance calls — and giving them a founding-member badge for saying yes early.

**Daily time commitment: 45 minutes.** No more. This is designed to fit around a solo founder's build/support schedule.

## 2. The Daily 45-Minute Routine

Run this exact block every day, Day 1–30. Same time daily (early morning or post-dinner works best — planners are on Instagram between 9–11 PM after client calls).

| Time       | Block                     | Action                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0–10 min  | **Warm-up scan**    | Reply to Instagram Story replies/DMs from previous day. Like + comment on 5 target agencies' latest posts (genuine, specific comments — never "Nice pic!"). |
| 10–25 min | **New outreach**    | Send 8–10 fresh Step-1 DMs (script below) to new names from your list.                                                                                      |
| 25–35 min | **Follow-ups**      | Send Step-2 or Step-3 DMs to anyone who opened/replied but went quiet (1–3 day follow-up cadence).                                                          |
| 35–45 min | **CRM + demo prep** | Update the tracker. If a demo is booked for tomorrow, note their specific pain point (from earlier DM) so you can personalize the walkthrough.               |

**Weekly non-negotiable add-on (30 min, once a week):** Post one piece of content on solo.founder.ai — a screen-recording tip, a "day in the life of a Nagpur wedding planner" relatable meme/story, or a founding-beta countdown ("18/25 spots left"). This turns cold DMs warmer because prospects check your profile before replying.

## 3. Week-by-Week Breakdown

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Warm the Well

- Focus 90% on Nagpur/Vidarbha — your home turf gives you credibility ("I'm building this from Nagpur, for Nagpur agencies").
- Goal: 60 Step-1 DMs sent, 15+ replies, 5 demos booked.
- Every DM should reference something hyper-local: a venue name, a recent wedding season pain point, or a mutual connection.

### Week 2 (Days 8–14): Convert Interest to Demos

- Shift ratio: 60% new outreach, 40% follow-ups + demo delivery.
- Goal: 8–10 demos delivered, first 3–5 founding agencies signed.
- Start dropping a soft scarcity line in DMs: "We've locked in 6 of the 25 founding spots this week."

### Week 3 (Days 15–21): Handle Objections, Push Pune Expansion

- Expand outreach to Pune agencies once Nagpur has 10+ signed (social proof: "Chitnavis Centre planners are already onboarding").
- This is peak objection week — most "I'll think about it" replies land here. Use the objection bank (Section 5) aggressively.
- Goal: 8 more signed, running total 13–15/25.

### Week 4 (Days 22–30): Close the Cohort + Referral Loop

- Introduce urgency: "Only X spots left, closing beta enrollment on [date]."
- Ask every signed founding agency: "Which 2 other agency owners do you trust most?" — warm referrals convert 3–5x better than cold DMs.
- Goal: Close remaining spots to hit 25/25. Any overflow goes on a "Cohort 2" waitlist — don't discard them.

## 4. The 3-Step DM Script Sequence

Keep every message under 4 lines. Planners read DMs on their phone between vendor calls — walls of text get ignored.

### Step 1 — The Opener (Day of first contact)

Goal: get curiosity, not a sale. Reference something specific and local.

> Hi [Name]! Saw the setup you did at [Venue name] last month — that floral stage was stunning. I'm building something for agencies like yours: a single app to replace the WhatsApp + Excel + Canva chaos during peak season. Building only for 25 founding agencies in Maharashtra right now, mostly Nagpur-first. Mind if I share a 60-second video?

*If no venue/post to reference, use:* "Saw you handle 15–20 weddings a year around Nagpur — that's exactly the size I'm building EventOS for."

### Step 2 — Follow-up (24–48 hrs later, if seen/no reply)

Goal: lower the ask, make it effortless.

> No pressure at all — I know wedding season DMs pile up fast. Quick context: EventOS auto-splits milestone payments (advance/pre-setup/post-event), sends WhatsApp payment reminders for you, and even works offline at lawns with zero signal. Want me to send a 2-min screen recording instead of a call?

### Step 3 — The Value Nudge (3–5 days later, if still quiet)

Goal: give value even without a reply — builds goodwill for a later re-approach.

> Totally understand if timing's off — season's brutal right now. One thing that might help regardless: most agencies lose track of pending vendor advances until it's a fire drill. Happy to send you a free milestone-payment tracker template either way, no strings. Let me know 🙏

*(If they engage with Step 3 but don't want the product yet, tag them "Nurture — retry Month 2" instead of dropping them.)*

## 6. The 15-Minute Demo Walkthrough

Structure every demo call the same way — consistency builds a repeatable close rate. Screen-share your own account with sample data pre-loaded (a fake ₹35L wedding works well).

**Minutes 0–2: Pain Confirmation**
Ask, don't pitch: "Walk me through what happens today when a lead comes in — WhatsApp? Excel? Where does it usually break down?" Let them talk. This tells you which of the 5 superpowers to emphasize.

**Minutes 2–5: The 60-Second Quotation**
Live-build a quotation in front of them (Catering, Décor, Sound/Light, Drone, GST) and send the digital sign-off link to your own phone. Show them signing it live. This is the single highest "wow" moment — always do it live, never just describe it.

**Minutes 5–9: Milestone Money & WhatsApp Automation**
Show the ledger splitting 50/30/20 across Advance/Pre-Setup/Post-Event. Then show a sample WhatsApp reminder firing 3 days before a due date. Say explicitly: "This is the message that replaces you calling a vendor asking 'sir, advance release karna hai kya.'"

**Minutes 9–12: Offline Field Ops**
Toggle airplane mode on your phone (do this live if possible) and show guest check-in/QR scan still working. Say: "This was built specifically because a Chitnavis Centre / open-lawn setup has dead 4G zones — I've heard this complaint from three agencies already."

**Minutes 12–14: White-Label Client Portal**
Show the branded subdomain and the watermark-to-full-res unlock gate tied to final payment. Frame it as: "No more sending final photos before the last invoice clears."

**Minutes 14–15: The Close**

> "You'd be one of our 25 founding agencies — that locks in lifetime discounted pricing, direct onboarding with me personally, and you get a say in what we build next. I can get you set up this week — want me to send the sign-up link now?"

Send the waitlist link immediately during the call, don't wait until after.

## 8. Founder Mindset Notes

- You will get ignored far more than you get replies — normal in B2B DM outreach (15–25% reply rate is healthy).
- Never discount the badge of "founding agency" — for boutique planners, being early and having direct founder access is a genuine status signal they'll use with their own clients ("we use custom event tech").
- Keep every demo personal. You are the product's credibility right now — not a sales deck.
