## 🎯 MONTH-BY-MONTH DETAILED ACTION PLAN

### 📍 Month 1: Public Beta Launch & First 10 Agencies
- **Task 1.1:** Execute 3 Final Verification Checks (SendGrid/SES OTP email delivery, Live webhook secrets, HTTPS SSL).
- **Task 1.2:** Flip production DNS records to `https://eventos.agency`.
- **Task 1.3:** Conduct direct outbound outreach to 50 wedding planners in Delhi-NCR & Mumbai using our 100-Lead Master Tracker.
- **Task 1.4:** Offer Founder Launch Pricing (Professional Plan at ₹5,999/mo with 14-day free trial).
- **Target Goal:** Onboard first **10 active paying agencies**.

### 📍 Month 2: Onboarding & Activation Optimization
- **Task 2.1:** Monitor 48-hour activation rate (creating 1st lead & generating 1st PDF quote).
- **Task 2.2:** Refine concierge onboarding scripts to eliminate setup friction.
- **Task 2.3:** Resolve any edge-case bug reports within 24 hours.
- **Target Goal:** Reach **65%+ 48-hour activation rate** and **0% trial churn**.

### 📍 Month 3: 25 Paying Customers & Referral Loops
- **Task 3.1:** Cross 25 active paying customer agencies.
- **Task 3.2:** Record and publish 3 video case studies of agency owners saving 40%+ team hours daily.
- **Task 3.3:** Launch agency-to-agency referral program (1 month free per referred paying agency).
- **Target Goal:** Reach **25 paying customers** and **₹1,50,000+ ($2,000) MRR**.

### 📍 Months 4 – 6: $1,000 – $3,000 MRR & Retention Focus
- **Task 4.1:** Scale inbound marketing (SEO blog guides, LinkedIn founder posts, 10-min YouTube walkthroughs).
- **Task 4.2:** Maintain Net Churn < 1% by conducting 30-day health checks.
- **Task 4.3:** Build ONLY features repeatedly requested by 5+ paying agencies.
- **Target Goal:** Reach **$1,000 – $3,000 (₹2,50,000+) MRR**.

### 📍 Months 6 – 12: 100+ Paying Agencies & Team Expansion
- **Task 6.1:** Scale to 100+ active paying event agencies across India and APAC.
- **Task 6.2:** Hire 1st Customer Success / Support Specialist to manage onboarding calls and tickets.
- **Task 6.3:** Upsell high-volume agencies to the Enterprise Tier (₹11,999/mo) for custom CNAME domains.
- **Target Goal:** Cross **100+ paying agencies (₹5,000,000+ / $6,000+ MRR | ARR ₹60 Lakhs+)**.
