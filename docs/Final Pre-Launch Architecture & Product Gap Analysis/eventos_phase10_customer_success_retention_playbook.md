## 🧮 3. CUSTOMER HEALTH SCORE ALGORITHM

$$\text{Health Score} = (\text{Usage Score} \times 0.40) + (\text{Business Value} \times 0.40) + (\text{Support Health} \times 0.20)$$

- **Usage Score (40 Points):** Login frequency (10 pts), Events created (10 pts), Quotes generated (10 pts), Team activity (10 pts).
- **Business Value (40 Points):** Total proposal volume managed (20 pts), active client bookings (20 pts).
- **Support Health (20 Points):** Open P1/P2 tickets (-10 pts per ticket), CSAT survey rating (10 pts).

## 🛑 4. CHURN PREVENTION & WIN-BACK CAMPAIGN

1. **Early Churn Signals:** Zero logins over 7 days, failed payment dunning retry, user export of client leads.
2. **Exit Survey:** Required 1-click survey when clicking Cancel ("Too expensive", "Missing feature", "Business closing", "Switched tool").
3. **Retention Offer:** Automatic 50% discount for 2 months offered to users canceling due to budget constraints.
