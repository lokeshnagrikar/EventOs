# Instagram Content Strategy: solo.founder.ai

This strategy plan outlines how to leverage the **EventOS** project to create engaging, viral, and business-focused content for your Instagram page.

---

## 📸 Content Categories & Video Scripts

### 1. The Aesthetic Hook (UI/UX Showcases)
* **Goal:** Attract other developers and tech enthusiasts to build your initial audience.
* **Visual:** Close-up screen recording of the **Apple Capsule Navbar** morphing on scroll or the **Bento Grid Spotlight Cards** following the cursor.
* **Audio:** Trending lo-fi or electronic beats (e.g., Synthwave/Ambient).
* **Text Overlay on Video:** *"Designing an Apple-style floating glass navbar for my new SaaS project."*
* **Caption Template:**
  ```text
  Building in public day [X]. I wanted the navigation experience on my new SaaS to feel like macOS. 

  Here is how I styled it:
  - bg-white/[0.03] backdrop-blur-2xl for transparent glass
  - border-white/[0.1] for the glossy edge
  - cubic-bezier ease-out scroll morphing

  What do you think of this layout? Drop a comment below! 👇
  #buildinpublic #solofounder #saasdev #uidesign #nextjs #tailwind
  ```

---

### 2. The Problem Solver (B2B Client Focus)
* **Goal:** Attract event planners and agency owners by highlighting their pain points.
* **Visual:** Record your screen showing how a lead is dragged to "Booked" on the Kanban board, which automatically generates a contract and a UPI QR Code invoice on the client portal.
* **Text Overlay on Video:** *"Stop manually emailing PDF invoices to clients. Do this instead."*
* **Caption Template:**
  ```text
  If you are running an event planning or wedding agency, chasing payments is a waste of time.

  I built EventOS to automate the entire client workflow:
  1. Drag a lead to "Booked" on your Kanban board.
  2. The system automatically creates a proposal.
  3. Your client signs online and pays instantly via UPI QR Code.

  No extra apps, no paperwork, no delays. 

  Link in bio to try the live demo! 🚀
  #eventplanner #weddingcoordinator #smallbusinessowners #saas #productivity
  ```

---

### 3. The Coding Journey (Dev Vlogs)
* **Goal:** Share raw developer insights and coding workflows.
* **Visual:** Time-lapse of you coding, or showing database schemas/Spring Boot backend architecture on screen.
* **Text Overlay on Video:** *"How I isolate database schemas for every tenant in my SaaS."*
* **Caption Template:**
  ```text
  Multi-tenancy is one of the hardest things to build in SaaS. 

  For EventOS, I chose to enforce strict schema-based database isolation. Each client's data is 100% isolated, meaning zero database leaks.

  Here is my stack:
  - Backend: Spring Boot & PostgreSQL
  - Frontend: Next.js & Tailwind CSS
  - State Management: Zustand

  Follow my page to watch me launch this live! 🛠️
  #indiehackers #springboot #postgres #backenddeveloper #fullstack
  ```
